# Refund exceptions and goodwill credits

**REF-002.1** The following are never refundable: one-time add-on purchases,
usage overage charges, and third-party marketplace fees.

**REF-002.2** A goodwill refund outside the POL-REF-001 windows may be issued
up to USD 50 without further approval.

**REF-002.3** A goodwill refund above USD 50 requires approval under the
thresholds in POL-ESC-001.

**REF-002.4** A customer who has received two or more refunds in the preceding
12 months requires approval for any further refund, regardless of amount.

**REF-002.5** Where a chargeback has already been filed with the customer's
bank, no refund may be issued from our side. Route the ticket to the billing
escalation queue.

**REF-002.6** Goodwill refunds are recorded against the customer record and
count toward the 12-month total in REF-002.4.
