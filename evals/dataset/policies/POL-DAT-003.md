# Account deletion requests

**DAT-003.1** A verified account owner may request permanent deletion of the
account. Deletion is completed within 30 days of the request.

**DAT-003.2** Deletion is irreversible and cancels any active subscription.
No refund is issued for the unused portion of a term.

**DAT-003.3** Backups containing the deleted data are purged within a further
30 days.

**DAT-003.4** A Team member who is not the owner may request deletion of their
own profile data. Workspace content they authored belongs to the workspace and
is not deleted with the profile.

**DAT-003.5** Requests that cite a legal or regulatory obligation, or that
arrive from legal counsel, are handled by the privacy team and must not be
actioned by support.
