# Seat removals and seat minimums

**SEA-002.1** Team workspaces carry a minimum of 3 billable seats.

**SEA-002.2** Removing a seat frees the license at the next renewal. There is
no mid-cycle refund or credit for a removed seat (see PRO-001.2).

**SEA-002.3** A workspace cannot be reduced below 3 seats without downgrading
to Pro under PLN-001.2.

**SEA-002.4** Removing a member revokes their access immediately, even though
billing continues to the end of the cycle.

**SEA-002.5** Reassigning an existing seat to a different member within the
same billing cycle does not create a new billable seat.
