# Tier entitlements

**TIE-001.1** Free: 1 seat, up to 3 projects, 500 MB of storage, community
support only.

**TIE-001.2** Pro: 1 seat, unlimited projects, 50 GB of storage, email
support.

**TIE-001.3** Team: 3 or more seats, unlimited projects, 500 GB of pooled
storage, shared workspaces, SSO, and priority support.

**TIE-001.4** API access is available on Pro and Team. It is not available on
Free.

**TIE-001.5** SSO and the audit log are Team-only features.
