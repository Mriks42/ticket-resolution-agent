# Proration on mid-cycle changes

**PRO-001.1** A mid-cycle upgrade is prorated: the customer is charged the
difference between the two plans for the remainder of the current cycle,
immediately.

**PRO-001.2** A mid-cycle downgrade produces no immediate charge and no
refund. The lower rate takes effect at the next renewal.

**PRO-001.3** Proration is computed on whole days remaining in the cycle. A
partial day counts as a full day in the customer's favour.

**PRO-001.4** On annual plans, upgrades are prorated across the remaining
whole months of the term. Downgrades take effect at renewal.

**PRO-001.5** Proration credits are applied to the account balance and offset
against the next invoice. They are not returned to the payment method while
the subscription remains active.
