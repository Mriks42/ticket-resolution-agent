# Support response targets

**SLA-001.1** Free: community forum only. No guaranteed response time.

**SLA-001.2** Pro: email support with a target first response within 1
business day.

**SLA-001.3** Team: priority support with a target first response within 4
business hours.

**SLA-001.4** Targets are measured Monday to Friday during business hours,
excluding public holidays.

**SLA-001.5** These are internal targets, not contractual service levels. No
service credits are issued for a missed response target.
