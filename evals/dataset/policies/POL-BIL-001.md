# Billing cycles and invoices

**BIL-001.1** Monthly subscriptions bill on the calendar day the subscription
started. In months without that day, billing falls on the last day of the
month.

**BIL-001.2** Annual subscriptions bill on the anniversary of the start date.

**BIL-001.3** Invoices are available under Billing → Invoices. VAT or GST is
added where the customer's billing country requires it.

**BIL-001.4** Invoice details such as company name and VAT ID can be corrected
by support for 60 days after issue. Later corrections require finance.

**BIL-001.5** A renewal notice is emailed 7 days before an annual renewal.
