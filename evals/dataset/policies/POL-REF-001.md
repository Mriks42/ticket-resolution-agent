# Refund window

**REF-001.1** Monthly Pro and Team subscriptions are refundable in full within
30 days of the charge being made.

**REF-001.2** The first term of an annual Pro or Team subscription is
refundable in full within 30 days of the initial charge.

**REF-001.3** Annual renewal charges are refundable within 14 days of the
renewal date.

**REF-001.4** The Free tier carries no charges and therefore no refunds.

**REF-001.5** Refunds are returned to the original payment method and settle
in 5–10 business days. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

**REF-001.6** Requests falling outside the windows above are not granted under
this policy. Discretionary exceptions are governed by POL-REF-002.
