# Data retention after downgrade or cancellation

**DAT-001.1** Data in an active Free account is retained for as long as the
account remains active.

**DAT-001.2** After a paid subscription is cancelled, workspace data is
retained for 90 days and then permanently deleted.

**DAT-001.3** After a downgrade to Free, content exceeding the Free tier
limits is made read-only for 30 days and is then deleted.

**DAT-001.4** Data in a suspended account is retained for the duration of the
suspension, subject to PAY-002.3.

**DAT-001.5** Deleted data cannot be restored, including from backups, once
the retention period has elapsed.
