# Plan downgrades

**PLN-001.1** A downgrade from Pro to Free takes effect at the end of the
current billing cycle. Pro features remain available until then.

**PLN-001.2** A downgrade from Team to Pro takes effect at the end of the
current billing cycle and requires the workspace to be reduced to a single
seat first (see POL-SEA-002).

**PLN-001.3** A downgrade does not trigger a refund or a mid-cycle credit;
see PRO-001.2.

**PLN-001.4** After a downgrade to Free, workspace content exceeding the Free
tier limits in POL-TIE-001 becomes read-only for 30 days before deletion, as
described in POL-DAT-001.

**PLN-001.5** Downgrades are an owner-only action. Admins and members cannot
change the plan.
