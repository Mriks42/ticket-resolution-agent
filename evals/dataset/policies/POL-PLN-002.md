# Plan upgrades

**PLN-002.1** Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated per
PRO-001.1.

**PLN-002.2** An upgrade from Free requires a valid payment method on file
before the change is applied.

**PLN-002.3** An upgrade to Team requires a minimum of 3 billable seats (see
SEA-002.1).

**PLN-002.4** Upgrading during an active trial ends the trial immediately and
charges the customer that day (see POL-TRI-001).

**PLN-002.5** If the upgrade charge fails, the account remains on its prior
plan and the charge is retried under POL-PAY-001.
