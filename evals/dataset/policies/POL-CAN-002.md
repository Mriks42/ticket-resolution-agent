# Cancellation during an annual term

**CAN-002.1** Cancellation of an annual subscription takes effect at the end
of the current annual term.

**CAN-002.2** Annual subscriptions are non-refundable once the term has
begun.

**CAN-002.3** The customer keeps full access for the remainder of the term
they have paid for.

**CAN-002.4** Switching from annual to monthly mid-term is not supported. The
change is scheduled for the end of the term.

**CAN-002.5** A renewal notice is emailed 7 days before the annual renewal
date, as described in BIL-001.5.
