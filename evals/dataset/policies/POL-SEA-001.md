# Seat additions

**SEA-001.1** A Team owner or admin may add seats at any time from Settings →
Members.

**SEA-001.2** Added seats are prorated per PRO-001.1 and charged immediately.

**SEA-001.3** Increases that take a workspace above 50 seats require a
sales-assisted contract and cannot be self-served.

**SEA-001.4** Pro is a single-seat plan. Adding a second seat requires an
upgrade to Team.

**SEA-001.5** A seat becomes billable on the date it is assigned, not the date
the invitation is accepted.
