# Cancellation of a monthly subscription

**CAN-001.1** A monthly subscription may be cancelled at any time. Access
continues to the end of the period already paid for.

**CAN-001.2** There are no partial-month refunds on monthly plans. A charge
may still be refundable under the window in REF-001.1.

**CAN-001.3** Cancellation is an owner-only action.

**CAN-001.4** Cancellation stops auto-renewal. The account moves to Free at
the end of the period.

**CAN-001.5** Data after cancellation is handled under POL-DAT-001.
