# Payment methods and currency

**BIL-002.1** We accept major credit and debit cards and PayPal. ACH transfer
is available for annual Team subscriptions of 10 seats or more.

**BIL-002.2** The billing currency is fixed when the subscription is first
created and cannot be changed on an existing subscription.

**BIL-002.3** A declined payment method is handled under POL-PAY-001.

**BIL-002.4** Purchase orders and invoice-based billing require an annual Team
contract arranged through sales.

**BIL-002.5** We do not store full card numbers. Card changes are made by the
customer in Billing → Payment method.
