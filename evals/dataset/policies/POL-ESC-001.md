# Approval thresholds and mandatory escalation

**ESC-001.1** Support may issue a refund of up to USD 50 without approval.

**ESC-001.2** A refund between USD 50.01 and USD 500 requires support manager
approval before it is issued.

**ESC-001.3** A refund above USD 500 requires finance approval.

**ESC-001.4** The following are escalated to a human regardless of amount:
suspected fraud, a chargeback already filed, any legal or regulatory demand,
reports of account takeover or unauthorised access, threats of litigation or
public complaint, and any request from someone not verified as the account
owner.

**ESC-001.5** Waiving an outstanding balance follows the same thresholds as a
refund of the same amount.

**ESC-001.6** Where policy is silent on the situation, or where two policies
give conflicting answers, escalate rather than improvising an answer.
