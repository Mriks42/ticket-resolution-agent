# Free trial terms

**TRI-001.1** New customers may start one 14-day Pro trial. No payment method
is required to begin it.

**TRI-001.2** At the end of the trial the account reverts to Free unless the
customer selects a paid plan.

**TRI-001.3** A trial may be extended at most once, by no more than 7 days.

**TRI-001.4** Team trials are arranged through sales and are not self-served.

**TRI-001.5** A customer who has previously held a trial on any account is not
eligible for a second trial.
